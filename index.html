01About

A gym built like a living system.

Himawari opened in 2014 with six barbells and a stubborn belief: the body is not a machine to be broken, it is a system to be trained. Strength first, then everything else — mobility, fuel, recovery — layered on top by coaches who know your name and your numbers.

We cap our classes small on purpose. We test every member every twelve weeks. And we program for the version of you six months from now, not the one chasing a sore body tomorrow. Discipline here feels less like punishment and more like tending something that grows.

  • 01
    Systems over hacks.

    Programs periodised in 12-week blocks — no random workouts, ever.

  • 02
    Progress you can measure.

    Strength, work capacity, mobility — tested quarterly, logged always.

  • 03
    Coaches who stay.

    Our average coach tenure is five years. Your plan never changes hands mid-story.
